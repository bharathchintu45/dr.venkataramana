Summary

Plant taxonomist with 20+ years of experience, 7 species newly described to science, 42+ peer-reviewed publications (111 citations, h-index 5), 6 authored/edited books, and 6 doctoral scholars supervised to completion.

Research interests

  • Plant Systematics. Studies on classification, taxonomy, morphology, and phylogenetic relationships of seed plants (Gymnosperms & Angiosperms).
  • Biodiversity & Conservation. Documenting biodiversity, preparing People's Biodiversity Registers, and developing actionable strategies for conservation of threatened flora.
  • Medicinal Botany & Ethnobotany. Research on indigenous medicinal plants, traditional healthcare knowledge systems, phytochemical screening, and botanical adulteration detection.
  • Indian Cycads Conservation. Specialized systematic, ecological, and conservation biology studies on the ancient gymnosperm genus Cycas L. in India.
